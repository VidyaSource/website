Legacy systems slow down more than IT. They slow down every process built on top of them, from customer approvals to safety reviews. Vidya has spent 6 years fixing that on systems millions of people depend on. Organizations that hire Vidya keep serving their current users while Vidya rebuilds the parts that are failing them.

## Why legacy modernization stalls

Rewriting code alone rarely fixes a legacy program. The data model underneath usually encodes the same assumptions the application does, so Vidya treats [data architecture](/consulting/software-architecture/) as part of the same job and decouples readers from writers before the services multiply. Delivery culture carries just as much weight. A team that ships once a quarter through a manual release cannot safely operate a modern architecture, so Vidya builds the agile practice and the [DevSecOps pipeline](/consulting/cloud-native-devsecops/) alongside the code.

The big-bang rewrite carries the most risk of all. A team freezes the old system and spends a year or more on a replacement. The gaps surface at cutover, when every user feels them at once.

## How Vidya modernizes a legacy system

1. **Name the risks first.** A 4-week assessment identifies your system's biggest risks and sequences the fixes so the riskiest change never comes first.
2. **Move one function at a time.** Vidya peels a single function out of the legacy system and runs the old and new versions side by side against the same live traffic.
3. **Retire the old path only after proof.** The old path stays in place until the new one has proven itself in production. Your users never see a service interruption.
4. **Modernize the data and the delivery process with the code.** Each step updates the data model and the release pipeline along with the application.
5. **Leave a team that owns the result.** Vidya documents why the system works the way it does and mentors the engineers who will maintain it.

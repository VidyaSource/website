## What federal programs need from a modernization partner

A federal service has to keep working for the public while it changes. People expect a passport application, a campsite reservation, or a health insurance plan to work on the day they need it. Every change must also meet Section 508 accessibility rules and run on infrastructure the agency has already authorized. A program office needs a partner who treats those constraints as the starting point of the design.
